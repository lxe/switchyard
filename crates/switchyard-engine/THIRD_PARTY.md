# Third-party components

Candle supplies tensors, linear algebra and prebuilt CUDA operations under its Apache-2.0 OR MIT license. Dependencies are pinned to commit `c68b24997319b8d76e5a4e775dab197c7f26322a`.

The Qwen3.5 text adapter in `src/models/qwen35.rs` is adapted from [Candle pull request 3396](https://github.com/huggingface/candle/pull/3396), commit `e9e4929f3e31b6183cc17e7be0596929c6725257`, by its contributors. It carries Candle's Apache-2.0 OR MIT licensing; the MIT permission notice is retained in `LICENSE-CANDLE-MIT`. Adaptations include partial rotary embeddings, F32 recurrent normalization/state, F32 source parameters and batched state isolation. This unmerged implementation is a starting point for numerical validation, not an upstream support guarantee.

The image encoder and merger in `src/models/vision.rs` adapt [Candle's Qwen3-VL vision implementation](https://github.com/huggingface/candle/blob/c68b24997319b8d76e5a4e775dab197c7f26322a/candle-transformers/src/models/qwen3_vl/vision.rs) at the pinned Candle revision. They retain the same Apache-2.0 OR MIT licensing and MIT permission notice. Qwen3.5-specific adaptations remove deep-stack output, use the released merger activation, map GGUF mmproj tensors and bound image preparation. Model mathematics and preprocessing are checked against the released Transformers implementation.

The GGUF layout adapter in `src/models/gguf.rs` follows the Qwen3.5 tensor contract documented by llama.cpp's MIT-licensed converter and model graph at commit `f2cc7282ce0042641df55e0e563b75e824e2b647`. It implements the tensor-name mapping, folded RMS weights, negative exponential decay and grouped/tiled value-head layout independently; packed arithmetic is supplied by the pinned Candle dependency. The offline `benchmarks/gguf_hf_reference.py` oracle uses llama.cpp's MIT `gguf-py` decoder and the published Transformers Qwen3.5 model implementation.
